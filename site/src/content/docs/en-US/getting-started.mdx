This guide takes you from zero to "first label printed" in under ten minutes. No signup, no cloud — everything runs locally on your machine.

## 1. Download and install

Pick the installer for your OS on the [download page](/bartender/download/):

- **Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)**: `Bartender_X.Y.Z_x64_pt-BR.msi`
- **macOS 12+ (Intel + Apple Silicon)**: `Bartender_X.Y.Z_universal.dmg`
- **Linux**: `bartender_X.Y.Z_amd64.deb` (Debian/Ubuntu) or `bartender_X.Y.Z_amd64.AppImage` (any x86_64 distro)

> **Unsigned on macOS.** On first launch, hold `Control` and click the app in Applications → **Open**. macOS remembers your choice after that.
> **Unsigned on Windows.** If SmartScreen warns, click "More info" → "Run anyway".

## 2. Meet the Gallery

When you open Bartender for the first time, you land in the **Gallery** — your library of label templates. From here you can:

- Create a new template from scratch (**+ New template**)
- Import an `.etlbl` archive (Bartender's portable format)
- Restore deleted templates from the **Trash**

## 3. Create your first template

Click **+ New template**. Configure:

1. **Name** — something like "Shelf label 50×30 mm".
2. **Dimensions** in millimeters — width × height. Physical size of your label stock.
3. **Printer density** (200 or 300 dpi) — check your printer's manual.

Bartender opens the editor with the canvas already sized to the right dimensions.

## 4. Design the label

Use the toolbar to add objects:

| Tool | Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | `T` | Text block with font, alignment, weight. |
| **Rectangle** | `R` | Rectangle with stroke and/or fill. |
| **Line** | `L` | Straight line at any angle. |
| **Ellipse** | `E` | Circle or ellipse. |
| **Image** | `I` | PNG / JPG / SVG from disk. |
| **Barcode** | `B` | 1D or 2D — see the [barcode reference](/bartender/docs/barcodes/). |

Every object can be dragged, resized, rotated and tuned via the **properties panel** on the right. Position and size are always in **millimeters**.

> **Tip:** hold `Shift` while dragging to lock to one axis. Use `Cmd/Ctrl + Z` to undo.

## 5. Save and head back to the Gallery

Bartender autosaves continuously (see [Autosave & recovery](/bartender/docs/autosave-recovery/)). To exit safely, click **Back to Gallery** in the top-left.

## 6. Print

From the Gallery, click the template and choose **Print**. The print dialog opens:

1. **Pick a printer** — any printer installed on your OS appears in the list.
2. **Density and margins** — tune if your printer has a physical offset.
3. **Preview** — confirm the result before sending.
4. **Print**.

For Argox or Zebra printers connected over USB/serial, Bartender offers **native commands** (PPLB or ZPL) — faster and sharper. See [OS-driver printing](/bartender/docs/printing/) or [PPLB & ZPL](/bartender/docs/pplb-zpl/).
