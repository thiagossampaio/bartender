Bartender supports two paths from app to label: the **operating system print driver** (this guide) and **native PPLB / ZPL commands** (see [PPLB & ZPL](/bartender/docs/pplb-zpl/)).

The OS-driver route works with **any printer** that shows up in your system's installed printers list — inkjet, laser or thermal.

## Print dialog

From the Gallery or the editor, click **Print** to open the dialog:

1. **Printer** — dropdown of every installed printer.
2. **Density** — render DPI. Default: 300 for thermal, 600 for laser. Match your printer's spec.
3. **Margins** — in mm. Use to compensate for physical printer offsets (see [Calibration](/bartender/docs/history-calibration/)).
4. **Quantity** — copies of the same label.
5. **Mode** — OS driver (this one) or native command (PPLB / ZPL, when applicable).
6. **Preview** — see exactly what will be printed.

## How it works internally

1. Bartender renders your label to an **off-screen canvas** at the chosen density.
2. The result is converted to a **raster bitmap** sized to the physical label.
3. The bitmap goes to the **OS spooler** (GDI on Windows, CUPS on Linux, Quartz on macOS).
4. The printer driver handles the final conversion to the printer's native protocol.

> **For thermal printers:** this route is slower and produces less sharp barcodes than sending native PPLB/ZPL. If your printer supports those commands and is connected via USB or serial, prefer [native commands](/bartender/docs/pplb-zpl/).

## Default printer

Set the default printer in **Settings** → **Printing** → **Default printer**. It is pre-selected in the print dialog, saving a click.

## Density — what to use?

| Printer | Recommended density |
|---|---|
| Thermal 200 dpi (Argox OS-214, Zebra GK420) | 200 |
| Thermal 300 dpi (Zebra ZD420, Argox CP-2140) | 300 |
| Office laser | 600 |
| Inkjet | 300–600 (printer manual) |

The density in Bartender should **match the printer's native density**. Lower density gives blurred edges; higher is wasted (the driver will resample).

## Preview

The preview shows the exact bitmap that will be sent, at the correct physical scale. Use it to:

- Check margins and crops.
- Confirm barcode sharpness (if the preview looks blurry, the print will too — raise the density).
- Spot text truncated by tight bounding boxes.

## Print settings

In **Settings** → **Printing**:

- **Default mode** — OS driver or native command.
- **Global margins** — applied to every print. Useful if your printer has a consistent physical offset.
- **Preview before printing** — toggle. If off, the app sends straight after confirming.
- **After printing** — open history, return to Gallery, or stay in the editor.

## Common issues

### "Printer doesn't appear in the list"

Bartender lists the same printers your OS does. If one is missing:

- Verify the printer is connected and on.
- Open **OS Settings** → **Printers** and confirm it's there.
- Restart Bartender (it scans at startup).

### "Output is blurry or pixelated"

- Raise the density in the print dialog to the printer's native density.
- Check that the OS driver is up to date.
- Try the [native command route](/bartender/docs/pplb-zpl/) if it's a thermal printer.

### "Shifts left / right / up"

Use the [calibration page](/bartender/docs/history-calibration/) to measure the offset and set corrective margins.
